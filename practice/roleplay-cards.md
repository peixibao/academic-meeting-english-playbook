# Role-play cards: realistic, unscripted practice

For every card: the simulated professor speaks first. The student should respond without reading. Practice twice with different wording.

| ID | Difficulty | Situation | Surprise turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | 1 | You enter a video meeting | Sound works but the professor speaks immediately |
| R02 | 1 | First greeting and small talk | Asked about your time zone |
| R03 | 2 | Tell me about yourself | Interrupted with a project question |
| R04 | 2 | Why this program and my group? | Professor asks you to be more specific |
| R05 | 2 | Explain one past research project | Asked what you personally did |
| R06 | 3 | Discuss a paper | Professor disputes your interpretation |
| R07 | 3 | Explore an MSc thesis | Professor says no topic is fixed yet |
| R08 | 3 | Discuss working style | Professor asks what supervision you need |
| R09 | 3 | Discuss publication potential | Authorship cannot be promised |
| R10 | 3 | Funding and formal eligibility | Professor sends you to graduate office |
| R11 | 4 | An unfamiliar method is introduced | You miss the acronym |
| R12 | 4 | Meeting is almost over | You must confirm the next step in 30 seconds |

## Mock meeting formats

- 5-minute survival round: R01–R03.
- 12-minute research exchange: R03, R05–R07, R11.
- 25-minute full prospective-supervisor conversation: R01–R12 selected naturally.
- Future lab meeting: progress, unexpected criticism, next steps.

## Feedback
Evaluate accuracy, naturalness, responsiveness, evidence integrity and ability to ask follow-up questions. Log two improvements, not twenty.
