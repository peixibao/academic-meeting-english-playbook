# 07 · Scheduling and follow-up

## Availability

“I'm available Monday through Wednesday between [START] and [END], [CITY] local time ([ZONE]). If another time is more convenient, I'd be happy to adjust.”

## Confirm

“Thank you. [DAY], [DATE] at [TIME] [ZONE] works well for me. I look forward to speaking with you.”

## Missing link

“Could you please share the meeting link when convenient?”

## Close naturally

“Thank you again. It was great learning more about your work. I appreciate your time.”

## Clarify next steps

“Just to confirm, should I [ACTION] before [DATE]?”

## Example thank-you email

Subject: Thank you for meeting with me

Dear Dr [SURNAME],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today. I enjoyed learning more about [PROJECT OR TOPIC]. Our discussion was particularly helpful in understanding [REAL INSIGHT].

As discussed, I will [AGREED ACTION]. Thank you again for your time.

Best regards,
[NAME]

Keep follow-up concise, specific and accurate. Do not claim a position or funding was offered unless explicitly confirmed.
