# 03 · Connecting a paper to research ideas

## Paper → insight → own experience → question

- “I came across your paper on [TOPIC].”
- “What caught my attention was [DESIGN / ANALYTICAL DECISION / LIMITATION].”
- “I encountered a related issue in a project where [YOUR VERIFIED EXPERIENCE].”
- “I'd be interested to hear how your team approaches [QUESTION].”

## Talk about thesis interests without overselling

“I have a few broad interests rather than a fully developed thesis proposal. I'm particularly interested in [METHOD] applied to [POPULATION / OUTCOME]. I'd be very open to developing a feasible question within an existing project.”

## Discuss methods naturally

- “What motivated the choice of that outcome?”
- “Would the dataset allow us to look at the question longitudinally?”
- “How is missingness usually addressed in this project?”
- “I can see why that approach is attractive. One concern I wondered about was ...”

## Follow the speaker

“That's interesting. Could you tell me a little more about [TERM/PROJECT]?” Then respond to their answer before shifting to the next question.

### Evidence precision

Identify protocol, observational study, trial, systematic review and methodological paper correctly; don't imply a published protocol already has study results.
