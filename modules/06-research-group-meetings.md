# 06 · Research group meetings (future)

## Progress update

“Since our last meeting, I've completed [TASK]. The main finding so far is [OBSERVATION]. I'm still working through [UNCERTAINTY], and I would appreciate feedback on [DECISION].”

## Clarify analytical choices

“One option would be [A], while another is [B]. The trade-off seems to be [TRADE-OFF]. Which approach would better match the research question?”

## Professional disagreement

“I see the reasoning behind that. Could we also consider [ALTERNATIVE], given [EVIDENCE / LIMITATION]?”

## Ask for help

“I've checked [STEPS ATTEMPTED], but I'm still uncertain about [SPECIFIC ISSUE]. Could you suggest how you'd approach it?”

## Recap a meeting

“So my next steps are [A] and [B], and I'll send the draft by [DATE]. Have I understood that correctly?”

### Adaptability
These patterns also work in journal clubs, collaborative manuscript discussions and lab meetings.
