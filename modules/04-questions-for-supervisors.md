# 04 · Questions for potential supervisors

Choose two or three relevant questions for a FIRST meeting; keep others for follow-up discussions. Avoid running through the whole page like an interview checklist.

## Most useful early

- “Are there ongoing projects that could be suitable for an MSc thesis?”
- “Would students have opportunities to help refine the research question and analysis plan?”
- “Would the thesis rely mainly on existing data, or also involve data collection?”
- “What skills do you hope your MSc students develop?”
- “If there seems to be a fit, what would you suggest as the next step?”

## Working style

- “How often do you usually meet with MSc students?”
- “How do you approach feedback on analyses or manuscript drafts?”
- “Would there be opportunities to work with other members of your group?”

## Publications and authorship

- “Do MSc thesis projects in your group sometimes lead to journal articles?”
- “How do you normally discuss publication plans and authorship expectations with students?”
- “At what stage do you clarify contributions and responsibilities?”

**Timing:** Ask after a particular project and expected student role become clear, or in a second discussion. No first-author guarantee follows simply from thesis enrollment. Biomedical authorship standards: https://icmje.org/recommendations/browse/roles-and-responsibilities/defining-the-role-of-authors-and-contributors.html

## Funding / admission

- “Does the program permit a self-funded thesis-based MSc?”
- “Are there department-level or supervisor-level funding requirements I should know about?”
- “Is there anyone in the graduate office whom you'd recommend I contact about eligibility?”

## Follow-up etiquette

“Could I ask one follow-up about that?” / “That answered one of my questions. I was also wondering ...”
