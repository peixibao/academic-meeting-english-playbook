# 02 · Your background and research

## A modular 30-second introduction

“I'm currently in the final year of a [DEGREE] at [INSTITUTION]. Most of my research experience has been in [RESEARCH TYPE], including work on [TOPIC A] and [TOPIC B]. Those projects have made me especially interested in [METHOD / OPEN QUESTION].”

## Expand to 90 seconds, if invited

Add your actual role: research question, participant recruitment, data quality, statistical analysis, interpretation, writing — only tasks you actually performed.

## Explain your project with 4 anchors

1. Question: “We wanted to understand whether ...”
2. Design/data: “We used [design] with [data source].”
3. Your own contribution: “I was responsible for ...”
4. Limitation and next interest: “One limitation was ..., which made me interested in ...”

## Helpful interactive turns

- “Would it be helpful if I gave you a quick example?”
- “The most challenging part was ...”
- “That experience made me more careful about ...”
- “I'm happy to go into the analysis in more detail if that's useful.”

### Scientific integrity

Avoid calling observational associations causal effects without defensible design and assumptions. Do not call an unfinished manuscript a published paper. Never read out the full CV uninvited.
