## Principle: modular, not memorized

A meeting is a two-way conversation. Assemble an answer from a communication purpose (greet, clarify, connect research, ask, summarize) rather than learning a fixed speech. The order of topics may change.

**Reusable core:** language functions and meeting skills in the modules below.
**Professor-specific layer:** paper-specific notes and prospective projects in a private copy of [Professor brief](templates/professor-brief-template.md). Do not publish actual emails, invitation URLs, unpublished study details or private assessments.

## Fixed eight-unit curriculum

This is a **systematic, comprehensive course of transferable English for academic and professional meetings**. Teaching order and coverage are determined by this syllabus; a learner saying "I have no questions" means they have no questions about the current section. It does not authorize changing the level, removing sections, creating new challenge tracks, or changing the purpose of the course.

1. **Lesson 01 — Joining & Greeting:** entering a call; tech check; first greetings; names and pronunciation; opening a meeting.
2. **Lesson 02 — Small Talk & Building Rapport:** social opening; reciprocal questions; developing topics; active listening and conversational transitions.
3. **Lesson 03 — Politeness & Academic Etiquette:** appropriate address; degrees of courtesy; requests; thanking; expressing regard and expectations.
4. **Lesson 04 — Turn-Taking & Conversation Management:** follow-ups, interruptions, topic changes, conversational pacing, and managing the floor.
5. **Lesson 05 — Unexpected Situations & Recovery:** mishearing, connection issues, clarification, thinking time, misunderstanding, and disruptions.
6. **Lesson 06 — Topic-Specific Discussion (replaceable):** prospective-supervisor and research talk, or later other meeting types; research examples supplement rather than replace the universal core.
7. **Lesson 07 — Expressing Expectations & Negotiating:** expressing needs, research alignment, priorities, boundaries, disagreement, and collaboration.
8. **Lesson 08 — Wrapping Up & Follow-up:** summary, next steps, gratitude, closing exchanges, and follow-up communication.

**Fixed teaching standards:** English-first explanations (Chinese only for relevant technical terminology), full conversational functions with alternative phrases and nuanced usage, realistic branching dialogue and unexpected situations, clean Markdown headings, note-friendly structure, no decorative boxes or compulsory beginner quizzes. Preserve all substantive material and proceed in syllabus order. The learner's questions trigger clarifications, not an unapproved curriculum redesign.

**GitHub update policy:** Check current files before teaching, store reviewed material, document edits, and **do not alter the approved syllabus based on perceived difficulty or the absence of questions**.

## Conversation arc

1. Set up the call and greet.
2. Make a concise introduction; listen to the professor's opening.
3. Connect a specific paper/method to your own experience.
4. Explore feasible research / thesis questions together.
5. Ask about supervision, contributions, training and mutual expectations.
6. Recap next steps, close, and follow up appropriately.

## Update rules

Read the latest version before editing; preserve previous phrases unless wrong or obsolete; record important changes in CHANGELOG; create a descriptive Git commit. GitHub is updated only when someone initiates an editing session, not continuously in the background.

## Safety and sharing

This is a PUBLIC teaching repository. Avoid actual private correspondence, meeting links, restricted data and recordings. Keep sensitive meeting-specific notes locally or in another private repository. Example conversations are fictional. Sources are linked and summarized; original external educational materials are not copied in full.

## Scope

This repository offers general communication practice. It does not establish any individual university's admission, funding, supervision or authorship rules. Verify those separately.
