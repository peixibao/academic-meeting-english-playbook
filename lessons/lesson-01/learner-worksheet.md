# Lesson 01 · Optional Practice Prompts

Use these after reading the detailed lesson notes. No multiple-choice exercises are required.

1. You join early, the professor initiates a tech check and apologizes for being two minutes late. Respond to all relevant conversational moves, concisely.
2. You are unsure about the professor's surname. Ask politely, check their model and accept correction.
3. The professor mispronounces your first name. Correct gently, model it, confirm.
4. The professor invites you to choose who begins. Offer a brief introduction and invite a response.
5. The professor asks for your **research background**, then changes to your **future interests**. Explain the difference in a few sentences.
6. The professor is short on time. Reprioritize what to discuss.

After practice, write down at most three expressions you used spontaneously and one situation that still feels difficult. Use the general progress log only for public-safe learning summaries.
