## Teaching and update rules

English-first, detailed language explanation, grammatical and pragmatic contrasts, register guidance, and branching professional conversations. Supplement technical terms in Chinese only as useful. Research examples are adaptable fictional examples, not a fixed learner profile. In 3.1 E2, distinguish current field interest from exploration of other fields, from specific research questions, and from research methodology itself.

Student questions are handled *within* the planned section; the existence or absence of questions does not authorize curriculum changes. Save a section after the learner reviews it; do not pre-save 3.2 or subsequent materials.
